TimeKeeper — Privacy Policy

Effective 18 September 2026

Your privacy matters, so this page explains plainly what TimeKeeper does and does not do with your information.

Data collection

TimeKeeper does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or usage data. The Activities, Activity Log entries, and settings you create are stored only in a local database on your own device. TimeKeeper does not request internet or network access and has no way to send any information off your device — not to me, and not to anyone else.

Advertising and analytics

TimeKeeper contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Your data, your control

Because everything stays on your device, you're always in control of it. Uninstalling the app removes its local data. TimeKeeper also lets you export your Activities list and your Activity Log / Summary data yourself, using the app's own Export features, if you'd like a copy — that data goes only wherever you choose to save or paste it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this same page with a new effective date.
