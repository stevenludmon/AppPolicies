ScoreKeeper Premium — Privacy Policy

Effective 18 September 2026

Your privacy matters, so this page explains plainly what ScoreKeeper Premium does and does not do with your information.

Data collection

ScoreKeeper Premium does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or usage data. The games, players, and history you create are stored only in a local database on your own device.

Advertising and analytics

ScoreKeeper Premium contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking of any kind — that's the whole point of Premium.

External links

The app's feedback option opens your device's email app, which leaves the app itself and is outside its control.

Your data, your control

Because your games and history stay on your device, you're always in control of them. Uninstalling the app removes its local data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this same page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to scorekeeper.premium@ludmon.com.au.
