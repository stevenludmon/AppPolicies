ScoreKeeper — Privacy Policy

Effective 18 September 2026

Your privacy matters, so this page explains plainly what ScoreKeeper does and does not do with your information.

Data collection

ScoreKeeper does not itself collect, transmit, or store any personal information on a server. The games, players, and history you create are stored only in a local database on your own device.

Advertising

ScoreKeeper is free to use and supported by ads served through Google AdMob. To show and measure those ads, Google's AdMob SDK may collect information such as your advertising identifier and general device information — this collection is done by Google, not by ScoreKeeper directly. You can read how Google handles this data in Google's Privacy Policy, and Android lets you reset or opt out of ad personalisation for your advertising ID in your device's settings.

External links

The app's "Go Premium" option opens the Play Store listing for ScoreKeeper Premium, and its feedback option opens your device's email app — both leave the app itself and are outside its control.

Your data, your control

Because your games and history stay on your device, you're always in control of them. Uninstalling the app removes its local data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this same page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to scorekeeper@ludmon.com.au.
